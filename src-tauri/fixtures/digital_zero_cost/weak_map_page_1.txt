1 A student investigates the resistance of a filament lamp. The lamp is connected in series with a
variable power supply, an ammeter and a voltmeter. The student records the current through the
lamp and the potential difference across the lamp for a range of supply settings.

(a) The student measures a current of 0.25 A when the potential difference across the lamp is
3.0 V. Calculate the resistance of the lamp. **[2 marks]**

(b) The student increases the potential difference and finds that the resistance of the lamp
increases. Explain, in terms of the ions in the filament, why the resistance increases. **[3 marks]**
